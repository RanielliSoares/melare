## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes architectural engineering authority with the tactile refinement of Apple-tier editorial digital flagships. Designed for high-end civil engineering, industrial development, and bespoke turnkey residences, the interface establishes an atmosphere of absolute precision, structural permanence, and quiet luxury.

### Aesthetic Philosophy
The visual direction blends **Ultra-Modern Dark Minimalist Glassmorphism** with **Editorial Architectural High-Contrast**:
- **Monolithic Obsidian Foundations:** Deep layered dark surfaces (`#0B0B0D`, `#121316`) evoke slate, honed black granite, and structural steel, eliminating glare while allowing project visuals to emerge with cinematic depth.
- **Champagne & Architectural Gold:** Warm metallic accents replace brash yellows, suggesting brushed brass fittings, precision surveying lasers, and evening interior illumination.
- **Atmospheric Optics:** 1px hairline micro-borders, refined background blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`), and gentle radial edge-highlights deliver depth without heavy skeuomorphic clutter.
- **Massive Typographic Scale:** Massive hero headlines command attention, framed by expansive negative space that mirrors the volume of high-ceilinged architectural spaces.

## Colors

The color palette is calibrated exclusively for a cinematic dark theme. Dominant surfaces utilize tinted obsidian hues that avoid pitch-black deadness, providing dimensional canvas layers for imagery and interface controls.

### Color Tiers & Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#D4AF37` / Gold Core):** Selected for primary conversion triggers, active indicators, structural accolades, and focal points.
- **Secondary (`#E5A93C` / Rich Warm Gold):** Used for hover states, visual gradations, and energetic accents in technical badges.
- **Tertiary (`#F5D77F` / Champagne Light):** Employed for illuminated edge rims, fine badge strokes, and micro-text accents that demand high-legibility sheen.
- **Neutral (`#0B0B0D` / Obsidian Canvas):** The base viewport background.
- **Surface Elevation (`#121316`, `#181A1E`):** Distinct structural slabs stacked above the base canvas with zero shadow dependency.
- **Typography Silver & Bone:** `#F5F5F7` provides glare-free primary contrast, while `#A1A1A6` and `#86868B` handle secondary narrative and engineering metadata respectively.

## Typography

The typography pairings mimic Cupertino’s ultra-rational modernist hierarchy. **Hanken Grotesk** serves as the headline and display standard—delivering razor-sharp terminal cuts, crisp geometries, and high-impact editorial presence without decorative distractions. **Inter** acts as the workhorse for body content, labels, specs, and navigational controls, engineered for uncompromised legibility on high-density OLED and retina displays.

### Typographic Rules
- **Negative Kerning at Scale:** Display headlines utilize tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to mimic poster design precision.
- **Technical Overlines:** Uppercase `label-caps` must precede primary project categories or milestone indicators (e.g., `FASE ESTRUTURAL`, `ALTO PADRÃO`), set with expanded tracking (`0.08em`) in `#A1A1A6` or `#D4AF37`.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Hero sections pair massive, thin-or-bold statements with relaxed body paragraphs constrained to a maximum width of 640px to ensure effortless eye scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an architectural cadence characterized by intentional breathing room, rhythmic baseline units, and rigid horizontal grid alignment.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column symmetrical fluid grid. Standard canvas width maxes out at `1440px`, with outer margins of `4rem` (or auto-centered on ultra-wide viewports) and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` outer margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters.

### Section Breathing Room
Hero sections and major construction showcase bands employ monumental vertical spacing (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl` / `6rem` to `9rem`), framing each architectural project as an individual exhibition piece. Content clusters and modular specification blocks leverage dense internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to establish self-contained structural cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained without muddy drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **luminous surface layering**, **glassmorphism**, and **hairline edge definition**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep obsidian ground (`#0B0B0D`), non-reflective and matte.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Slabs & Cards):** `#121316` surface finished with a continuous `1px solid #26262B` perimeter stroke.
3. **Level 2 (Floating Glass & Navigational Capsules):** Translucent obsidian base (`rgba(18, 19, 22, 0.72)`) backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(160%)` and reinforced with a crisp top-edge highlight (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
4. **Level 3 (Interactive Active States / Overlays):** Elevated modals and active cards introduce a soft champagne ambient glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 40px -10px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(245, 215, 127, 0.2)`.

### Border Discipline
Never apply thick borders. Structural framing must exclusively rely on `1px` hairlines to preserve architectural drawing elegance.

## Shapes

The geometry strikes a calculated equilibrium between structural architectural precision and ergonomic digital touchpoints.

### Corner Treatment
- **Core Cards & Containers:** Standardized at `rounded-md` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) to `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`), maintaining the disciplined silhouette of structural blueprint slabs.
- **Hero Image Viewports & Showcase Portals:** Set to `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to present high-resolution photography with gallery-standard containment.
- **Controls & Metadata Badges:** Navigational capsules, tag chips, and primary action buttons utilize full circular pills (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to contrast against rectilinear card layouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Gold Monolith):** Background `#D4AF37`, text `#0B0B0D` (Inter SemiBold), fully rounded pill or `rounded-lg`. Hover brings subtle brightness scaling (`#E5A93C`) and a diffused perimeter halo (`0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Action (Obsidian Glass):** Background `rgba(24, 26, 30, 0.8)`, text `#F5F5F7`, border `1px solid #26262B`. Hover state shifts border to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)` and background to `#181A1E`.
- **Text / Editorial Action:** Monospaced or bold label with trailing arrow (`→`), transitioning from `#A1A1A6` to `#F5D77F` with a 2px horizontal translate on hover.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Structural Construction Card:** Base `#121316`, `1px solid #26262B`, `rounded-lg`. Houses full-bleed photography with a subtle gradient ramp fading to obsidian at the bottom.
- **Feature Spec Card:** Dense dark container with technical specs (`m²`, timeline, location, phase indicator). Uses a micro champagne divider (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).

### Input Fields
- Dark slate fill (`#0B0B0D`), border `1px solid #26262B`, text `#F5F5F7`, placeholder `#86868B`, radius `0.5rem`.
- Focus state activates an illuminated hairline border: `1px solid #D4AF37` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).

### Chips & Status Badges
- Semi-translucent obsidian pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), uppercase micro-typography (`label-caps`).
- Phase badges (e.g., *FUNDAÇÃO*, *ALVENARIA*, *ENTREGA*) feature a 6px glowing status dot (Gold `#D4AF37` for active, White `#F5F5F7` for completed).

### Architectural Stage Stepper
- Horizontal evolution rail illustrating construction progress from ground breaking to key handover. Uncompleted steps render in `#26262B` hairlines; completed and current phases glow with `#D4AF37` brushed gold progress tracks.
